Logos of the bill providers (cable TV, betting, exam pins, internet), converted to PNG and trimmed.
Every logo is the property of its respective company; it is used here only to identify that provider on bill screens and receipts.

Where each one came from
  nairabet, betway, sportybet, smile, spectranet
      "NigeriaLogos" - https://github.com/PaystackHQ/nigerialogos - MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019 Paystack (text below)
  dstv (2023 logo), showmax, 1xbet
      Wikimedia Commons, files marked public domain by their uploaders:
      "DStv Logo (2023).svg", "Showmax Logo.svg", "1xBet-Logo.png"
  waec (crest)
      Wikimedia Commons "Waec logo.png", licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
      (converted to a smaller PNG; the crest itself is WAEC's emblem)
  gotv, startimes, betking, naijabet, bangbet, betland, jamb
      The company's own website (site icon / logo file): gotvafrica.com, startimestv.com, betking.com, naijabet.com,
      bangbet.com, betland.com, jamb.gov.ng. BetLand's yellow wordmark is set on BetLand's own dark brand colour (#1C2021).
  merrybet
      No logo yet - MerryBet shows an initials tile until a file is added.
