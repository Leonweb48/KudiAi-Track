## 12. YOUR DATA RIGHTS

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### 12.1 Right of Access
You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.

### 12.2 Right to Rectification
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data. You may update most of your data directly through the Platform settings.

### 12.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in the following circumstances:
- The data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
- You withdraw consent (where consent was the legal basis).
- You object to processing based on legitimate interests and there are no overriding interests.
- The data has been unlawfully processed.

Note: We may retain data where required by applicable law (e.g., financial records) or for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

### 12.4 Right to Restriction of Processing
You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your data (meaning we store it but do not actively use it) where:
- You contest the accuracy of the data.
- Processing is unlawful but you prefer restriction over deletion.
- We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims.
- You have objected to processing pending verification of our legitimate grounds.

### 12.5 Right to Data Portability
You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV), where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means.

### 12.6 Right to Object
You have the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. We will cease such processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms.

### 12.7 Right to Withdraw Consent
Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or updating your notification/marketing preferences in the Platform. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 12.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint
If you believe we have processed your personal data in violation of the NDPA 2023, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**:

Nigeria Data Protection Commission  
Website: ndpc.gov.ng  
Email: info@ndpc.gov.ng

We encourage you to contact us first so we can address your concern directly.

**How to Exercise Your Rights:**  
Submit a written request to: support@kudiai.app (Subject: "Data Rights Request")  
We will respond within **30 days** of receipt, or notify you of an extension (up to a further 60 days) where justified by the complexity of the request.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

## 13. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:

**Technical Measures:**
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data in transit (TLS/HTTPS).
- Encrypted data storage via Supabase PostgreSQL (AES-256 at rest).
- Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access their own data.
- JWT-based authentication with secure cookie storage.
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) for Admin Portal and Marketer Portal access.
- reCAPTCHA v3 bot protection on registration and login.
- Automated session logging for anomaly detection.
- Security alerts for suspicious login activity.

**Organisational Measures:**
- Access to personal data limited to personnel with a business need.
- Staff data protection awareness.
- Third-party processors vetted and bound by data processing agreements.
- Incident response procedures.

**Limitations:**  
No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal data using commercially reasonable standards, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the NDPC in accordance with the NDPA 2023's breach notification requirements (within 72 hours of becoming aware, where feasible).

## 20. GLOSSARY

- **NDPA 2023** — Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
- **NDPC** — Nigeria Data Protection Commission
- **NDPR 2019** — Nigeria Data Protection Regulation 2019 (predecessor regulation)
- **Data Controller** — The person or entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data
- **Data Processor** — The person or entity that processes personal data on behalf of the controller
- **Personal Data** — Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **Sensitive Personal Data** — Data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, biometric data, financial data, or other categories specified under the NDPA 2023
- **Processing** — Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion
- **NIN** — National Identification Number (Nigerian government identity document)
- **KYC** — Know Your Customer (identity verification procedures)
- **FIRS** — Federal Inland Revenue Service (Nigerian tax authority)
- **CAMA 2020** — Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020
